# Quantization Error As Aesthetic

Quantization error is the difference between what an image wants to be and what the system can actually represent.

Dithering turns that error into visible structure.

## Without dithering

A limited palette creates:

- ugly banding
- flat posterization
- abrupt color jumps
- missing intermediate tones

## With dithering

The same limitation becomes:

- granular transition
- texture
- apparent intermediate value
- rhythm
- material behavior
- machine personality

## Style principle

Do not hide the error completely.

The point is not to pretend the limitation does not exist.
The point is to make the limitation speak in patterns.

## Visual metaphor

Dither is a negotiation between scarcity and illusion.

It says:

```text
I cannot give you gray.
But I can give you black-white-black-white-black-white until your eye invents gray for me.
```

That is the whole sacred scam.
